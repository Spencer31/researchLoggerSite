Help with Research Logger

Support

Find quick answers below or get in touch. When writing, include what you were trying to do and the device or operating-system version involved—but please do not send private research content.

iCloud sync

Confirm that you are signed into iCloud and that iCloud access is enabled for Research Logger on each device.

Attachments

Research Logger keeps an attachment with its journal entry and synchronizes it through your private iCloud storage when sync is available.

Usage analytics

You can change Share Anonymous Usage at any time in Research Logger Settings. Core app features work without analytics.

Deleting data

Delete entries, projects, tasks, and attachments within the app. Synced changes are reflected through iCloud on your other devices.
